About

Small Space Setup publishes buying-consideration guides for home office and work-from-home gear aimed at renters, apartment dwellers, and anyone working from a small or shared room. We focus on desks, storage, cable management, chairs, and accessories that fit tight footprints and rental restrictions (no drilling, easy to move).

We don't publish first-hand product reviews or invented performance claims. Our articles explain what to evaluate and why, so you can make an informed choice regardless of which specific product you land on.
